Ismael Sierra

About me

My research interests lie at the intersection of algebraic topology, geometric topology, and algebraic geometry. I am particularly interested in algebraic K-theory, Ek-algebras, homological stability phenomena, the Goncharov program, cohomology of linear and arithmetic groups, and moduli spaces of manifolds.
